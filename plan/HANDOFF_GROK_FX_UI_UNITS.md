## Rules for every sheet
- Regenerate if: any lettering or numbers, a fake signature, a fake transparent checkerboard, objects touching each other or the sheet edge, or wrong colours.
- Keep the 1–2 best of each. Don't edit or upscale.
- Union = strong, clearly saturated Union blue coats, sky-blue trousers (must read blue when shrunk). Confederate = grey and butternut, slouch hats.
- Flags: none on these sheets.
